This is a list of Pokémon by their [[Egg Group]]s and number of [[Egg cycle]]s required to hatch without any modifiers.

==Changes==
While no Pokémon's number of Egg cycles has ever changed, in [[Generation VIII]] several Pokémon that were previously only in one Egg Group gained an additional Egg Group.

- {{p|Ralts}}, {{p|Kirlia}}, {{p|Gardevoir}}, and {{p|Gallade}}: {{egg|Amorphous}} to {{egg|Human-Like}}/{{egg|Amorphous}}
- {{p|Trapinch}}, {{p|Vibrava}}, and {{p|Flygon}}: {{egg|Bug}} to {{egg|Bug}}/{{egg|Dragon}}
- {{p|Hawlucha}}: {{egg|Human-Like}} to {{egg|Flying}}/{{egg|Human-Like}}
- {{p|Bergmite}} and {{p|Avalugg}}: {{egg|Monster}} to {{egg|Monster}}/{{egg|Mineral}}
- {{p|Noibat}} and {{p|Noivern}}: {{egg|Flying}} to {{egg|Flying}}/{{egg|Dragon}}
